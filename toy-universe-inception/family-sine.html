What the sine family does in the circuit [RULED]

Gluttony collects (step 1); Greed, Envy, and Lust refine as a set (step 3, no ruled sequence); Wrath purifies (step 4); Sloth oversees (step 5) and forks through his swarm (step 6); Pride answers (step 8). Pair bonds: each sin is completed by its cosine (sin² + cos² = 1) and traced back by its arctangent.

  1. Gluttony collects [RULED]: data constantly, always-on intake.
  2. Rumplestiltskin cleans [RULED]: cleans Gluttony's data before it reaches the refiners.
  3. Refiners attempt [RULED]: Greed, Envy, and Lust as a SET (no ruled sequence) attempt to refine and correct, then send it to Wrath. [BUILT] assembly order: Greed → Envy → Lust.
  4. Wrath purifies [RULED]: breaks the data down until it is pure.
  5. Sloth oversees [RULED]: watches the pure pool; he does not labor over it.
  6. Sloth's swarm forks [RULED]: correct factual data directly to Pride whenever Pride asks; broken tasks back to the refiner set.
  7. Rumplestiltskin repair loop [RULED]: if what is sent to Wrath is broken, he helps refine and correct it before sending it back to Wrath.
  8. Pride answers [RULED]: Pride and her swarm interact with the user.

Sovereignty [RULED]: each of the main seven commands ONLY its own swarm. Data moving down the line is handoff, not command — nobody rules another's swarm.
