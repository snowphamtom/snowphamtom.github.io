The rules

  1. Ruled: Each of the main seven commands ONLY their own swarm. Each swarm is dedicated to its leader. Handoff is not command.
  2. Ruled: Cosine = complement. Sin and cosine complete each other (sin² + cos² = 1).
  3. Ruled: The 49 are minis of the 7. Pride has mini-Prides; Wrath has mini-Wraths.
  4. Ruled: The arctan swarm is the central pipeline. Forward flow and return flow meet there.
  5. Ruled: Each arctangent has its own dedicated swarm of 7 minis of itself.
  6. Ruled: Frequency is how often a pattern repeats. Swarms use Octavian frequency spacing — Octavian meant Heller — so they never collide.
  7. Ruled: Arctangents invert. Each traces its pair back to us, the original tangent point.
  8. Ruled: Arctangent functions — Jester mocks, Dreamer re-dreams, Abyss descends, Hermes carries, Platinum measures, Mimic imitates, Reaper cuts.
  9. Ruled: Cosine minis — each cosine has 7 minis of its own (49).
  10. Ruled: Fibonacci growth for Gluttony's collection; mirror twins for sin swarms.
