The Charter

THE FLOOR — CHARTER

Repo: snowphamtom/the-swarm (PRIVATE — the swarm interior is confidential; the impenetrable posture governs everything in this repo). Design of record: Optimal_Bot_Swarm_Design_2026-10-22.html (Monsters Ink LLC). Seats of record: seats.json v1.1, adopted 2026-10-22. Spine: the register — an append-only, hash-chained ledger. The VM copy is canonical; register/register.jsonl in this repo is its verified mirror.

The admission law (the GATE) — Taylor's text, unchanged

The swarm's admission law is the refusal law, unchanged: measure what is left over after an interaction; admit only what fits inside that leftover; otherwise refuse and record the refusal.

A task is admitted to a seat only if it fits that seat's leftover — capacity, evidence, authority. Everything else is refused and the refusal is recorded.

How the floor works on GitHub

  • One issue per seat. A seat's issue is its thread and its charter: the

body pins the seat's wing, occupant, cadence, capacity, and scopes from seats.json. No seat speaks without its issue.

  • Admission events (a packet admitted, a packet refused, a receipt) are

recorded as comments on the seat's issue and as rows in the register. The register is the proof; the issue is the window.

  • Labels are the state. seat:<id> names the seat, wing:<name> its

wing, state:live|structure|admitted|refused|unresolved its standing. A seat whose state label changes gets a comment saying why, in the same act.

  • The HIVE is a wing of this floor, not a second repo — one floor, per

the design of record. Its nine seats carry wing:hive + hive:<name>. Four live through their successors on the floor; four are structure-only; the ninth seat is UNRESOLVED (NH-10) and is never invented.

  • TOLL's limits carry over exactly: TOLL cannot sign, send, submit, or

spend. Its scopes here are what they are on the VM: sweep, adjudicate, stage.

  • Standing grants (from seats.json): ALW-GITHUB-T2RZZ7 (github-write,

to 2026-11-19), ALW-GMAIL-LCNRUX (gmail, to 2026-11-19), ALL-ACCESS-2026-10-22. Disclosure-class scopes are never covered by a standing grant: publishing unfiled matter needs Taylor's per-action decision.

What is live, with and without the Actions workflows

The Muse GitHub App can read/write issues, comments, labels, and ordinary files here — the floor runs on those powers alone from its first push. The App cannot create this repo (403, measured 2026-10-23) and cannot push workflow files (the Workflows permission is Taylor's item T1, ungranted).

  • Live without workflows: every seat issue and its thread; label state;

the register mirror as files (pushed by the VM mirror script under the house push lock); Taylor's window and hand through Working Copy.

  • Added when Taylor pushes .github/workflows/ from Working Copy:

register-mirror-check re-verifies the mirrored chain on a 30-minute rhythm and comments on the register issue if the chain ever breaks; seat-heartbeat posts a cycle-state comment on the FLOW seat issue hourly. Until then, the VM's own crons (FLOW 30-minute sweep, the keeper watchdog) remain the rhythm, exactly as they are today.

Durability — why this repo exists

The VM that runs the swarm dies to platform churn roughly hourly; the register, the keeper, and the reaper record prove it. This repo is the durable substrate: the register mirror means the chain's verified state survives any single VM death and is readable from Taylor's phone. If the VM register and this mirror ever disagree, the mirror's REGISTER_STATE.json names the last verified tip — the chain is re-derived from the VM original, never from the mirror alone.
