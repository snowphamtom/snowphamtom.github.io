The Charter

The admission law, in Taylor’s words, unchanged:

The swarm's admission law is the refusal law, unchanged: measure what is left over after an interaction; admit only what fits inside that leftover; otherwise refuse and record the refusal.

A task is admitted to a seat only if it fits that seat's leftover — capacity, evidence, authority. Everything else is refused and the refusal is recorded.

  • One floor. The swarm and its HIVE wing work on a single floor, under one admission law.
  • Admission. A task reaches a seat only if it fits that seat’s leftover — capacity, evidence, authority. Everything else is refused, and the refusal is recorded.
  • The register is the spine. Every admission, refusal, and receipt is a row in an append-only, hash-chained ledger. The register is the proof; this floor is the window.
  • Standing limits. TOLL cannot sign, send, submit, or spend. It sweeps, adjudicates, and stages.
  • Durability. The register is mirrored to this floor and re-verified at every publication, so the record survives any single machine.
