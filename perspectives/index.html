The doctrine in one page

The statement. All of recorded human history is just rearranging individual unique perspectives. (Taylor Heller, 2026-10-23.)

The terms. A perspective is one unique held source — no single is the same. Recorded means admitted into the record through the gate. A rearrangement is a composition: held perspectives crossed (the Combinator; mechanically, the creative frontier’s lattice — 57 complexes × 4 shapes = 228 positions over the current six-domain pantry, 20 filled and 208 open at this writing).

The measure. A rearrangement is an event if and only if it leaves a leftover: novel data units — entities, multi-digit figures, and bigrams present in the composition and absent from every other held source, prior admissions included — numbering at least 1. Zero leftover is a reshuffle: refused, and the refusal recorded. The leftover is computed (pipeline_value v3’s newness definition), never asserted.

The gate. Four checks, refusal the default at each: PALETTE (every element a held perspective) · TRACE (every numeral cites its source fragment) · LEFTOVER (novel units ≥ 1; the crossing itself unminted) · AIM (the composition names a held question). Admission admits a candidate, never a fact.

The value form. Value = new data × field consequence. The new-data term is the leftover, measured. The consequence term is observed externally and is UNRESOLVED as a derivation — do not compute it from inside the record and do not assert it. “Value is knowledge. Price is belief.”

The operating loop. Read the record → propose → submit to the gate → record the verdict either way. The register (476 rows at this writing, hash-chained) is the record of which rearrangements were admitted and which refused; it does not rewrite itself.

The boundary. The doctrine governs the record, not the unrecorded. What is not held is a named gap (the NOT-HELD ledger), never a conclusion.

The ceiling. Possible events grow combinatorially with held perspectives — positions = 4 × Σ C(n,k), k = 2..6 — so widening the pantry (all possible sources) raises the ceiling faster than the pantry grows; and over a fixed pantry the measured leftover shrinks toward the threshold as admissions accumulate, which is why new perspectives, not more reshuffling, are the growth act.

1 · The statement and its terms

The statement has three working terms. Each is defined here from the record, not from the dictionary.

PERSPECTIVE — one unique held source, one unique instance. The atom of the doctrine is a single held item: a source, a fragment, a domain, a walk — held in the record and distinct from every other. Taylor’s mathematical language for the atom, on record since 2026-10-16: “no single is the same” — every instance is distinct. In the creative faculty’s current mechanical form, the held perspectives are the Combinator’s pantry: six domains (AI Safety · Codec / C2 · Maritime · Space · Spatial / VR · T&E Assurance) harvested from the record itself. A perspective that is not held is not in the doctrine’s universe: the gate’s PALETTE check refuses any composition naming a domain outside the held pantry (see §2).

RECORDED — admitted into the record through the gate. “Recorded” in the statement is not “written down somewhere.” In this system a thing is recorded when it has passed the gate and been entered in the record — the register, the ledgers, the admitted corpus — where it is hash-chained and cannot quietly rewrite itself (law L10, the skeleton law: the record “is the skeleton: frozen, certificate-checked, and it never self-edits”). What was never submitted, or was submitted and refused, is not part of recorded history in the doctrine’s sense — except that the refusal itself is recorded, with the same permanence as an admission (“What does not fit is refused, and the refusal is recorded”).

REARRANGEMENT — a composition: perspectives crossed. A rearrangement takes two or more held perspectives and crosses them into a candidate that none of them is alone. The Combinator is the house’s name for the operation (assets “composed by crossing the record’s own domains (the Combinator method)”). Its current mechanical form is the creative frontier’s simplicial lattice: crossings of the six held domains at orders k = 2..6, in four shapes. A rearrangement is a proposal. It carries no standing until the gate disposes of it (§2).

So the statement, in the record’s own vocabulary: everything in the record is a composition of distinct held sources, admitted because the composition left something new.

2 · The measure — event vs reshuffle

Most rearrangements change nothing: the same content in a new order. The doctrine’s whole weight sits on the test that separates a rearrangement that is an event from one that is a reshuffle. The test is the canonical line (law L3):

Measure what is left over after an interaction; admit only what fits inside that leftover; otherwise refuse and record the refusal.

The leftover (☆), defined and computed. The leftover of a composition is its novel data units — data units present in the composition and absent from every other held source. The definition is pipeline_value.py v3’s, verbatim from its header:

NEWNESS = novel data units / total data units — where a “data unit” is a distinctive entity, a multi-digit figure, or a bigram, and “novel” means absent from every other source in the corpus.

The creative gate computes the same quantity against the entire held corpus plus all prior admitted rows: a composition’s novelty is measured against everything the record already holds, including its own earlier admissions. The event test is a threshold on that count: novel_units ≥ 1. A rearrangement with zero novel data units is a reshuffle by measurement, not by opinion — refused, and the refusal recorded.

The gate’s four checks, mapped onto the doctrine. The frontier disposes of every candidate through four laws. In doctrine terms:

  • PALETTE — only held perspectives rearrange. Every element of the composition must be a perspective the record actually holds. A composition naming an unheld perspective is refused before any measurement of its content: it is not a rearrangement of the record, it is an import.
  • TRACE — every element cites its perspective. Every numeral in the composition must trace to a cited fragment of a held source. An untraced figure is an element with no perspective behind it — refused.
  • LEFTOVER — the event test. Novel data units ≥ 1 against the held corpus and all prior admissions, and the (perspectives × shape) crossing itself unminted. This is the doctrine’s measure: pass, and the rearrangement is an event; fail, and it is a reshuffle.
  • AIM — rearrangement toward a held question, not drift. The composition must name a live target held in the opportunity queue. A novel composition aimed at nothing the record is asking is refused: newness without a question is drift, and the doctrine admits events, not drift.

Refusal is the default state at every check (“refusal is the default… the burden is always on the proposal”). Admission admits a candidate, never a fact.

3 · The value law as corollary

Taylor’s value law is the doctrine priced (law L8; Taylor’s words 2026-10-17: “value is based off of the data is new”):

Value = new data × field consequence. “Value is knowledge. Price is belief.”

Read through §2, the corollary is exact: the new data term is the leftover — the novel data units a rearrangement leaves, the same count the gate measures. A reshuffle has a new-data term of zero, so its value under the law is zero whatever its other term is; that is the doctrine’s §2 test restated as arithmetic. An event has a positive first term; its value then scales with the second.

The second term carries its standing label, kept honest: the field-consequence term is UNRESOLVED as a derivation. pipeline_value.py v3 states it in its own header: the field/domain effect “is NOT measurable from inside the corpus (it requires observing the field, not the text). It remains an explicit external input, default UNRESOLVED.” Every row of the creative ledger carries the same line in its consequence field: “UNRESOLVED — observed externally.” The doctrine therefore prices an event’s first term exactly and its second term not at all: what the field does with admitted new data is observed after the fact, never computed in advance and never asserted.

4.4 The register itself

The register is the doctrine’s ledger of itself: 476 rows at build, hash-chained, each row an admission, a refusal, a decision, or a receipt — the running record of which rearrangements were admitted and which refused, in order, none of it editable after the fact (L10). The creative ledger is the same form one level down: 39 rows — 1 BIRTH, 10 CYCLE markers, 20 ADMITTED, 8 REFUSED — and its verify mode re-gates every historical row from the ledger alone, so the record of verdicts is itself verifiable, not just stored.

The ceiling on possible events is the position count, and the position count grows combinatorially in the number of held perspectives: positions = 4 × Σ C(n,k) for k = 2..6 over n perspectives. [EXTENSION] Applied to a widened pantry — the source expansion Taylor ordered 2026-10-23 15:50 CT (“Include all possible sources, including stories, poems, and plays”) and widened at 15:52–15:53 CT — the same formula at n = 12 perspectives gives 4 × (66 + 220 + 495 + 792 + 924) = 9,988 positions. The doctrine’s reading: each new held perspective does not add its own crossings one at a time; it crosses with every combination already held, so the space of possible events grows faster than the pantry. This is why “all possible sources” is a ceiling-raising act and not a filing act. The widened pantry’s actual kind count is being established by the expansion crew’s census; until it lands, n = 12 above is an illustration of the formula, not a count of the record.

5.2 The base rate — what the ledger actually shows

Of the 28 compositions gated to date: 20 admitted, 8 refused (admission rate 20/28 = 0.714; refusal rate 8/28 = 0.286). Two qualifications, both measured, both load-bearing:

  • All 8 refusals are designed gate vectors (V1 outside-palette, V2 untraced figure, V3 zero-leftover duplicate, V4 unaimed — run once in the skeleton era, seqs 8–11, and once in the frontier era, seqs 25–28). They exist to prove the gate refuses. Of the 20 organic cycle compositions gated, 20 were admitted — because the frontier’s rotation ranks candidate crossings by Marginal Residual Density (novel_units / total_units) and proposes the highest-leftover crossings first. The gated sample is selected, not drawn at random from the 228 positions; the 0.714 rate is the rate among pre-ranked proposals, and says nothing direct about the 208 unproposed positions.
  • The leftover is shrinking as the record fills. Novelty is measured against the held corpus plus all prior admissions, so every admission raises the comparison set for the next composition. The measured sequence shows it: the first admission carried 166 novel units (ratio 0.943182); admissions at seqs 30, 32, and 36 carried 3 novel units each (ratios 0.016216, 0.018405, 0.019737). [EXTENSION] The doctrine’s prediction, stated as a prediction: over a fixed pantry, continued minting drives the typical leftover toward the threshold — rearrangements of the same perspectives increasingly become reshuffles — and the only moves that restore headroom are new perspectives (a wider pantry, §5.1) and higher-order crossings (the unfilled k = 3..6 positions, 168 of the 228). The ledger’s trend line is consistent with this; the trend is 28 gated compositions deep, and the prediction stands or falls on the next cycles’ measured ratios.

5.3 The boundary

The doctrine governs the record: held perspectives, gated rearrangements, recorded verdicts. It says nothing about what was never held. An unrecorded perspective — a source never copied in (L4), a walk never run, a refusal never submitted — is outside the doctrine’s universe, not denied by it. Inside the system, the standing name for that outside is the NOT-HELD ledger: each item labeled DERIVED, HOUSE, TAYLOR, or OPEN — gaps stated as gaps, never filled by the doctrine’s framing. The statement says “recorded human history”; the doctrine keeps the adjective load-bearing.
