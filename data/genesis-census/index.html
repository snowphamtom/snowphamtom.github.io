GENESIS Census — Open Data

Taylor Heller / Monsters Ink LLC. The complete measured census of the 48-node GENESIS system: 1,128 pairs classified 944 DOOR · 124 ALIAS · 60 NEVER; 7,140 doors; 83 distinct pair sums; 17 complement pairs.

  • pairs.csv — all 1,128 pairs with class and doors on pair
  • doors.csv — all 7,140 doors
  • W_pair_adjacency.csv — the 48×48 pair-adjacency matrix
  • midpoints.csv — the 83 pair sums
  • spectra.txt — W and T spectra
